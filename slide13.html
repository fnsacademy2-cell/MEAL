MEAL Complements Financial Controls and Internal Audit

MEAL serves as a second line of defense against operational and reputational risks.

1
FINANCE
  • Focus: Budget line compliance, allowable expenditures, and receipts.
  • Key Checks: Invoices, vouchers, payroll taxes, bank reconciliations.
  • Limitation: Cannot verify if the named patient physically received care.
2
MEAL (Operational Defense)
  • Focus: Physical service delivery, targeting eligibility, and quality of care.
  • Key Checks: Field spot-checks, recipient identity audits, user satisfaction.
  • Limitation: Does not audit institutional general financial ledgers.
3
INTERNAL AUDIT
  • Focus: Independent governance assurance and overall enterprise risk.
  • Key Checks: Procurement integrity, anti-fraud controls, board policies.
  • Role: Synthesizes Finance and MEAL reports to assess control health.
🛡️
PRIORITY RISKS CONTROLLED BY MEAL: Ghost Beneficiaries (spot-checking existence) • Duplication (stopping cross-centre double counting) • Elite Capture (verifying poverty criteria) • Data Inflation (primary document audits).
