Theory of Change and Logic Models Form the Strategic Core

The strategic blueprint connecting project intent to verified results.

🔬
THEORY OF CHANGE
The Causal Hypothesis
“Why will these interventions lead to sustainable impact?”
  • Explains the Causal Pathway: How clinical activities address the root causes of disease and exclusion.
  • Maps Preconditions: Sequence of intermediate behavioral and social changes required for success.
  • Explicit Assumptions: Defines external socio-economic, clinical, and environmental variables.
📐
LOGIC MODEL & RESULTS FRAMEWORK
The Operational Blueprint
“What measurable evidence proves the intended change occurred?”
  • Operational Structure: Systematically maps Inputs → Outputs → Outcomes → Impact.
  • Measurement Standards: Sets SMART indicator targets, verification baselines, and milestone checkpoints.
  • Risk Control: Integrates operational assumptions and early-warning monitoring triggers.
🎯
THE STRATEGIC PAIR: Theory of Change provides the intellectual hypothesis; the Logic Model provides the operational measurement blueprint.
