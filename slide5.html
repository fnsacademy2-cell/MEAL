The Four Pillars Function as One Integrated System

Four pillars operating as one integrated management system.

M
MONITORING
“Are we on track?”
  • Routine tracking of inputs, activities & budget
  • Early bottleneck detection for rapid adjustment
  • Ensures operational milestone compliance
E
EVALUATION
“Did it work & why?”
  • Periodic assessment of impact & relevance
  • Verifies outcomes attributable to MALC
  • Evaluates long-term sustainability
A
ACCOUNTABILITY
“Are we answerable?”
  • Patient dignity, transparency & 'Do No Harm'
  • Safe, accessible beneficiary feedback channels
  • Community participation in service review
L
LEARNING
“What will we change?”
  • Translates evidence into better clinical practice
  • Continuous institutional reflection
  • Prevents recurring operational errors
⚡
THE MEAL INTEGRATION: Monitoring & Evaluation generate credible evidence • Accountability & Learning turn evidence into continuous clinical improvement.
