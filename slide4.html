THE MANAGEMENT CASE Slide 4 of 19

Turning Mission into Measurable Change

Connecting field evidence directly to strategic decision-making.

🎯
MISSION
“Are we fulfilling our mandate?”
  • Aligns outreach to core vision
  • Improves patients' lives directly
  • Focuses on marginalized cohorts
📊
EVIDENCE
“Do verified outcomes prove change?”
  • Measures outcomes, not just efforts
  • Verified data replaces assumptions
  • Audit-ready proof for partners
🤝
ACCOUNTABILITY
“Are communities heard & safe?”
  • Dignified, non-discriminatory care
  • Safe, accessible complaint desks
  • Upholding the 'Do No Harm' pledge
🔄
IMPROVEMENT
“Do we adapt when evidence requires it?”
  • Adapts clinical practices quickly
  • Closes feedback loops permanently
  • Institutionalizes shared learning
💡
THE CENTRAL STRATEGIC SHIFT: Moving from “What did we do?” (activities) → to “What changed, for whom, at what cost—and what will we improve?” (impact & learning).
