Addressing the Core Challenges in MEAL Implementation

Practical solutions for common operational field bottlenecks.

01
LIMITED BUDGET
  • Challenge: Constrained budgets & lack of field staff.
  • Fix: Ring-fence 3–5% MEAL budget; appoint clinical focal points.
02
DATA ERRORS
  • Challenge: Incomplete paper registers & typos.
  • Fix: Deploy mobile forms (Kobo); mandate quarterly 10% DQA.
03
TIME CONSTRAINTS
  • Challenge: Documentation perceived as diverting care.
  • Fix: Streamline indicators to essentials; automate reporting.
04
STANDARDIZATION
  • Challenge: Fragmented metrics across donors.
  • Fix: Institutionalize standard SOPs & unified Indicator Dictionary.
05
FEAR OF FEEDBACK
  • Challenge: Fear of punitive action from errors.
  • Fix: Foster a blame-free learning culture focused on improvement.
🛠️
OPERATIONAL EXCELLENCE: Addressing field bottlenecks requires practical simplification, supportive leadership, and ring-fenced operational resources.
