Collecting High-Quality Quantitative and Qualitative Data

Combining numerical metrics with qualitative patient experience.

🔢
QUANTITATIVE DATA
Monitoring Scale
  • Sources: Standard clinical registers, attendance logs, and mobile intake forms.
  • Focus: Routine tracking of patient volumes, device fittings, and clinical follow-ups.
  • Validation: Mobile logic in KoboToolbox eliminates entry errors and double counting.
💬
QUALITATIVE EVIDENCE
Evaluation & Context
  • Sources: Focus Groups (FGDs), Key Informant Interviews, and exit surveys.
  • Focus: Care dignity, accessibility barriers, and Most Significant Change (MSC) stories.
  • Value: Explains why results happen and catches unintended consequences.
🛡️
ETHICS & SAFEGUARDING
Beneficiary Protection
  • Informed Consent: Explicit voluntary consent obtained prior to interviews or recordings.
  • Data Protection: Strict anonymization, encrypted storage, and data minimization.
  • Dignity: Respectful community engagement protecting vulnerable patient groups.
⚖️
THE GOLDEN RULE OF DATA: “Garbage In, Garbage Out.” No statistical software can fix flawed field data. Disciplined collection protocols are the bedrock of trustworthy evidence.
