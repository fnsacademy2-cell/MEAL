Reliable Evidence Demands Disciplined Data Quality Standards

Data Quality Assessment ensures reported figures reflect true field reality.

1
ACCURATE

Data accurately mirrors field reality with zero bias, rounding errors, or statistical inflation.

2
COMPLETE

All mandatory fields, catchment districts, and patient demographics are fully recorded without gaps.

3
TIMELY

Information is collected, validated, and reported on schedule to guide real-time clinical decisions.

4
CONSISTENT

Zero contradictions exist between paper clinic registers, monthly summaries, and central databases.

5
RELIABLE

Standardized indicator protocols produce identical findings regardless of which officer performs the count.

6
VALID

Measurement tools genuinely capture the specific clinical or social construct claimed—nothing else.

🔍
ROUTINE DQA AUDIT PROTOCOL: Quarterly 10% sample recount of registers against database • Physical patient spot-checks • Continuous validation rule calibration.
