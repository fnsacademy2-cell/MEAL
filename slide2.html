What is MEAL?

A systematic framework to track progress, measure impact, and continuously improve performance.

MEAL is a systematic organizational framework used to track progress, measure impact, and continuously improve project performance across all clinical and field interventions:

M
MONITORING
“Stay on Course”
  • Routine tracking of daily activities, clinical inputs, and direct outputs.
  • Observes ongoing progress against approved budgets and timelines.
  • Detects operational bottlenecks early to enable immediate course-correction.
E
EVALUATION
“Measure Impact”
  • Periodic, rigorous assessment of project relevance, effectiveness, and efficiency.
  • Determines whether long-term changes are truly attributable to MALC's work.
  • Assesses sustainability and value for money for partners and donors.
A
ACCOUNTABILITY
“Open & Responsive”
  • Transparent, two-way communication with communities and patients.
  • Safe, accessible, and confidential feedback & complaint mechanisms (CFM).
  • Upholds patient rights, dignity, and the fundamental principle of 'Do No Harm'.
L
LEARNING
“Act on Insights”
  • Turning monitoring data, evaluations, and complaints into actionable improvements.
  • Refining clinical SOPs, treatment pathways, and future project designs.
  • Fostering an institutional culture of continuous reflection and evidence-based growth.
💡
CORE ESSENCE: MEAL connects field reality directly to leadership decision-making—transforming daily activities into verified, lasting life transformation.
