ACCESSIBLE INTAKE CHANNELS
  • Dedicated toll-free helpline & WhatsApp communication line
  • Locked, tamper-proof suggestion boxes at all static health clinics
  • Community helpdesks active during remote field outreach camps
  • Anonymous whistleblowing channel for sensitive safeguarding allegations
📜
CORE OPERATING PRINCIPLES
  • Confidentiality & Safety: Absolute protection of whistleblower identity.
  • Strict Service SLAs: Acknowledged in 48h; investigated & resolved in 14 days.
  • Non-Retaliation: Zero negative impact on beneficiary care or future access.
  • Closing the Loop: Mandatory feedback provided directly back to the complainant.
📢
STRATEGIC VALUE OF FEEDBACK: “A complaint is diagnostic evidence of a programme flaw.” Recurring complaints regarding waiting times, device fit, or staff conduct trigger leadership reviews.
